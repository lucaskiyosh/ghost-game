Challenge

Suspicious of the AI? Press Challenge. It has to show a word that starts with the letters on screen:

  • if it does, you lose;

The AI can challenge you too. Then type the rest of the word you had in mind and press ENTER.

Levels & dictionary

Easy, Medium and Hard change how many moves ahead the AI looks, how many words it knows and how much it bluffs.

Dictionary: the 50,000 most frequent words in movie subtitles (FrequencyWords, built from OpenSubtitles), keeping only those in the SCOWL American English word list (wamerican), with 4 or more letters.

We count visits and games anonymously, without cookies (GoatCounter).

Daily challenge

Every day there's a new challenge, the same for everyone: 3 rounds against the AI, each harder than the last (Easy, Medium, Hard). The AI opens each round with a letter.

At the end, share your score without spoiling the words, and come back tomorrow to keep your streak 🔥. Want to train? Use the Practice tab, which doesn't count for the daily.
