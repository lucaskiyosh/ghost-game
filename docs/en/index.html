How to play

You and the AI take turns adding one letter. Whoever completes a word of 4 or more letters loses.

Your letters are yellow and the AI's are green. Here the AI completed "home" and lost.

Bluffing is allowed: you can play a letter that doesn't start any word. If you suspect the AI, press Challenge: it has to show a word that starts with the letters on screen. If it does, you lose; if it was bluffing, you win.

The AI can challenge you too. Then type the rest of a word and press ENTER.
